        Here, even offline

        Getting ready for offline use...

        Installation and updates

        After a successful first online visit, Phase can load offline. Where supported, use your browser's Install or Add to Home Screen action. Only app files are cached, never your diary or exports.

        Phase checks for updates on opening and when you return. Choose 'Restart to update' when you're ready. Minimizing the app leaves the update waiting. Restart keeps your records but clears Undo. Other open windows do not restart automatically.

        Phase cannot diagnose health conditions. Do not rely on its estimates for contraception.
