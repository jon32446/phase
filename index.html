        Settings and privacy

        Phase stores your records in this browser on this device. It has no analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party runtime resources, and does not send your records to anyone.

        Browser storage is not a backup and can be lost. Keep your own JSON backups. Exports are unencrypted; secure your device and exported files.

        Backup and export

        JSON includes daily observations, period starts, history reviews, and check-in dismissals. CSV includes daily observations and period-start markers only, not reviews or dismissals, and cannot be restored here. Unrecorded days are omitted, not exported as none.

        Restore a JSON backup

        Restore replaces your entire diary, including reviews and dismissals. It does not merge records and cannot be undone. Export your current diary first if you want to keep it. The file is read on this device, never uploaded.

        Delete saved data

        Delete all Phase records, history reviews, and check-in dismissals in this browser. Downloaded exports and records in other browsers or devices are not deleted.

        GitHub Pages receives ordinary website-request metadata. Other apps on the same origin could technically access this storage. The owner promises that other apps they publish there will not read or share your records; use a separate origin if you do not want to rely on that promise.

        Offline use and updates

        Preparing offline use...

        Where supported, use your browser's Install or Add to Home Screen action to open Phase as an app. Offline use needs a successful first online visit; only application files are cached, never your diary or exports.

        Phase checks for updates when opened and when you return to it. An update waits for you to choose Restart to update; minimizing the app does not apply it. Restart clears session Undo but keeps saved records. Other open windows are not automatically restarted.

        This is a diary, not a diagnostic tool or a method of contraception. There are no accounts or cloud sync.

        Do not include real health records in public issues or screenshots.
