    Settings and privacy

    Phase stores your records in this browser on this device. It has no analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party runtime resources, and does not send your records to anyone.

    Browser storage is not a backup and can be lost. Backup/restore, CSV export, and the in-app delete-all control are not implemented in this development version. You can clear this site's data using your browser settings; doing so may also delete data belonging to other apps on the same origin.

    GitHub Pages receives ordinary website-request metadata. Other apps on the same origin could technically access this storage. The owner promises that other apps they publish there will not read or share your records; use a separate origin if you do not want to rely on that promise.

    This is a diary, not a diagnostic tool or a method of contraception. There are no accounts or cloud sync. Offline installation is not available yet.

    Do not include real health records in public issues or screenshots.

    Not recorded is different from no bleeding. Saving this day does not fill in neighbouring days.

    A period start requires light, medium, or heavy bleeding.

    Only conflicting dates are listed below. Approve each period-start removal to save the whole batch, or go back to change the selection or action. No selected day changes until the entire batch saves.
